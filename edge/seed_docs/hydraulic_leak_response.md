# Hydraulic leak response

## Immediate actions
1. Stop the affected hydraulic consumer if safe to do so (crane, windlass, hatch cover).
2. Barricade the spill area; warn crew of slip hazard.
3. Fit a drip tray or absorbent pads under the leak.
4. Notify the officer of the watch and the chief engineer.

## Containment
- Do not wash oil overboard. Collect with oil-absorbent materials.
- Isolate the circuit with local valves when isolation is safe.
- Tag out the equipment until repaired.

## Repair and follow-up
- Replace damaged hose or fitting with the correct pressure-rated spare.
- Pressure-test before returning to service.
- File a near-miss / hazardous situation report in Beacon.
- Check sister equipment for the same hose routing and drip-tray fit.
