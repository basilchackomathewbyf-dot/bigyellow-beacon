# Enclosed space entry

## Purpose
Safe entry into ballast tanks, cargo tanks, cofferdams and other enclosed spaces.

## Before entry
1. Permit to work issued by the officer in charge.
2. Atmosphere tested for oxygen (19.5–23.5%), flammable gas (<1% LEL) and toxic gases.
3. Ventilation running for at least 15 minutes before and during entry.
4. Standby person posted at the entrance with communication to the bridge.
5. Rescue equipment (BA, stretcher, harness) staged at the entrance.

## During entry
- Continuous gas monitoring with a personal meter.
- Exit immediately if the meter alarms or communication is lost.
- No lone working in enclosed spaces.

## After exit
- Close the space, restore blanks, cancel the permit, and record the entry in the log.
